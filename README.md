# 🏥 MediNexus AI

### Intelligent Healthcare Management System with Disease Prediction & Medical Report Analysis

MediNexus AI is an AI-powered healthcare management platform designed to connect **Patients, Doctors, and Hospital Administrators** through a centralized and real-time healthcare ecosystem.

The system combines **AI-based disease prediction, medical report analysis, centralized patient management, secure role-based access, REST APIs, and WebSocket-based real-time synchronization**.


## 🚀 Key Features

$ 👤 Patient Portal
- Secure patient registration and authentication
- Personal healthcare profile
- Symptom-based disease prediction
- Medical report and document management
- Previous prediction and clinical history
- Doctor assignment and communication

$ 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Portal
- Secure doctor authentication
- View assigned patients
- Access patient clinical information
- Review previous predictions and reports
- Manage patient prescriptions and consultations
- Real-time patient assignment notifications

$ 🏥 Hospital Admin Portal
- Centralized patient management
- Comprehensive Patient Log
- Doctor registration and pre-authorization
- Doctor-patient assignment
- Hospital operation management
- Patient risk and prediction monitoring
- Real-time notifications

$ 🤖 AI & Prediction

The system provides AI-assisted disease prediction based on patient-provided symptoms and available medical information.

The prediction workflow includes:

Patient Symptoms
       ↓
Data Processing
       ↓
AI/ML Prediction
       ↓
Disease Risk Evaluation
       ↓
Recommended Medical Specialist
